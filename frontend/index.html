Continuity engine

Expected care and recorded care stay visibly aligned.

दाई compares configurable pathway templates against actual events and turns missing follow-up into clear, assignable operational tasks.

  • Configurable stage-based pathways per program.
  • Gap provenance: expected item, data source and flag timestamp.
  • Operational outreach priority, never clinical risk scoring.

Context aggregation

Doctors walk in with facts, not generated clinical conclusions.

The brief organizes dates, documents, values and missing-information flags from structured and unstructured sources without interpreting what they mean clinically.

  • Extracted facts carry source and confidence metadata.
  • Missing or inconsistent information is shown explicitly.
  • No autonomous diagnosis, treatment suggestion or clinical summary.

Clinic flow

Operations support that keeps staff in control.

दाई supports interruption-heavy front-desk workflows with suggestions, alerts and logged schedule changes. Urgency is only shown when staff explicitly set it.

Why दाई

Built with safety constraints in the architecture.

1

Assistive, not diagnostic

दाई surfaces facts, gaps and drafts. It does not diagnose, recommend treatment or score clinical risk.

2

Human override

AI-touched output must be reviewed, edited, rejected or approved before it affects downstream work.

3

Audit trail

Important actions are logged with who, what, when and affected entity for operational traceability.

4

Caregiver-friendly

Non-clinician touchpoints are designed for clear next steps, multilingual use and low training.
