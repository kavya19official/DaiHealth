# Ultrasound demonstration image credits

The images in this directory are real obstetric ultrasound frames used ONLY for
demonstration purposes in the DaiHealth hackathon demo. They are NOT scans of any
DaiHealth patient and carry no clinical meaning in this context.

Source dataset: FETAL_PLANES_DB — Common maternal-fetal ultrasound images
(Burgos-Artizzu et al., 2020), Zenodo record 3904280, Version 1.0.
DOI: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.3904280
Mirrored via Hugging Face: https://huggingface.co/datasets/chehablab/FETAL_PLANES_DB

License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Attribution: Burgos-Artizzu, X.P., Coronado-Gutierrez, D., Valenzuela-Alcaraz, B.,
Bonet-Carne, E., Eixarch, E., Crispi, F. and Gratacós, E. (2020). FETAL_PLANES_DB:
Common maternal-fetal ultrasound images (1.0) [Data set]. Zenodo.

Images were converted from JPEG to PNG without other modification. Plane labels
come from the dataset's expert annotations:

- brain_1012.png  — Patient01012, Fetal brain plane
- brain_1013.png  — Patient01013, Fetal brain plane
- abdomen_1427.png — Patient01427, Fetal abdomen plane
- thorax_1466.png — Patient01466, Fetal thorax plane
- femur_1135.png  — Patient01135, Fetal femur plane
- profile_14.png  — Patient00014, Other plane (general fetal view)
