# Nivara → Hemangini → Nivara video rehearsal

Use synthetic records only. Open patient and doctor in separate browser profiles (or normal and incognito). Two ordinary tabs share the same login cookie and are unsuitable for two roles.

Nivara: G2 P1 L1, singleton pregnancy, LMP 11 May 2026, assigned EDD 15 February 2027; 28 weeks 2 days on the reference date 25 November 2026. Aarav is her one born child (18 May 2024). Do not create a duplicate patient or a second born-child record for the current pregnancy.

## The connected story

1. Patient: Home → My care → Pending care. Explain the next recorded follow-up, then open Appointments → Book a visit. Choose Mother, Antenatal check-up, Hemangini, an available clinic day/time, and a short note. Review and submit. Status is requested/pending, not a guaranteed slot.
2. Doctor: Appointments. Refresh if needed, select the same requested date, find Nivara's new request, then Confirm schedule. Add a short clinician note. Patient: return to Appointments and refresh. The same visit now shows confirmed. This is the first two-way loop.
3. Patient: Reports & records → Upload external report. Use a labelled fictional report; select Mother and the correct report type/date. Upload, then explicitly share with Hemangini. Doctor: My patients → Nivara → Reports & results, refresh, search the test, open the original. Reading a PDF does not automatically verify its extracted values or complete a milestone.
4. Doctor: review/verify a report using the existing clinician review controls. Any edit to a clinical value or care milestone requires the clinician's explicit saved action. Return to the patient's reports/timeline and refresh to show the released or saved record. Do not promise that an unreviewed upload changed clinical care automatically.
5. Patient: Messages → Hemangini → send a short follow-up question. Doctor: Messages → Nivara → reply. Return to the patient conversation and show the saved reply. Routine messages are not an emergency service.
6. Optional live call: doctor opens Requests, sets availability to Available and clicks Enable call alerts once. Patient: SOS → Request doctor call → describe a fictional concern, choose English/Hindi/Marathi and explicitly consent → Send request. Doctor receives a visible incoming request and an audible alert when browser audio is allowed. Accept, then both sides choose Join audio call and allow microphones. Actual media requires both participants, devices and network permissions; acceptance and connectivity are never guaranteed. A visible request is not proof that a clinician has already responded.
7. Doctor can finish the accepted consultation with the existing reason, observations, guidance, outcome and follow-up controls. Once released/completed, the patient sees it in Doctor Connect history. A draft or an unsubmitted note must not be shown as released advice.

Appointments poll while their patient page is open (about 15 seconds); messages poll while visible (about 5 seconds); consultation requests poll about every 2.5 seconds. Refresh controls can remove waiting from the recorded demo.

## Supporting scenes

- Home / care timeline / birth readiness: context and practical preparation. Show the recorded stage and explain pending items.
- Pregnancy mirror: enable the camera intentionally. Present this as a camera/visualisation demonstration, not a fetal measurement or diagnosis.
- Uterus model: educational anatomy, not the patient's live anatomy.
- My children → Step into Aarav's world: stays in the patient workspace. Birth details, saved vaccine entries, record-count charts, growth/development, visits and child-specific reports are separate from the pregnancy. Missing vaccination entries do not prove a missed dose.
- Ultrasound: full upload panel above a compact history. Expand a scan, then AI frame analysis or released clinician report. Public dataset images and scripted measurements remain labelled demos; SonoNet frame analysis does not establish a diagnosis for Nivara.
- Family: cartoon portraits represent Aarav, Kabir and Ritu. Support responsibilities are separate from granted data permissions.
- Daily tracking: log only a clearly labelled demo entry. Sukoon is an intentionally private wellbeing area; do not imply that private journalling is silently shared with the doctor. Pregnancy Library is the patient's keepsake space.

## ABHA / DigiLocker scene

At an ABDM-enabled lab, the patient asks for the report to be linked to her ABHA address and approves the relevant linking/sharing flow. A participating lab must actually issue/link the record; an ABHA number alone does not retrieve all reports. DAI's current ABDM/DigiLocker controls are labelled connection demos with fictional PDFs because approved production/sandbox partner access is not connected. Show patient consent, physician access and revocation as the working prototype. Do not claim a live government fetch.

## SOS scope and evidence

SOS offers three independent choices: call emergency services, request a doctor consultation / open care messages, or optionally describe symptoms. No questionnaire or app login is required to open the emergency dialler. The app does not dispatch an ambulance. Do not call real emergency numbers during rehearsal.

Questions capture pain location, character, severity, onset, pattern, response to rest and additional warning signs. Skipped answers remain unknown. They do not diagnose labour, ligament pain, infection, pre-eclampsia or psychological pain, and do not produce an all-clear. Severe symptoms show immediate contact guidance before submission. Backend emergency rules recompute the selected symptom warnings when a summary is saved. The prototype question set requires clinical validation before clinical deployment.
