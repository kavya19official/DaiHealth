The care loop

How Anvaya works

Most health apps stop at a reminder. Anvaya runs a loop that only closes once the care has actually happened.

  1. 1

    Care gap

    The expected pathway for this stage is compared against what has actually been recorded, and anything overdue or missing surfaces.

  2. 2

    Action

    The gap becomes one clear instruction — what is needed, why it matters now, and what to ask the doctor about it.

  3. 3

    Access

    The family is pointed to a suitable gynaecologist, paediatrician, PHC, CHC, lab or vaccination centre — and any government scheme that applies.

  4. 4

    Completion

    The visit, report or vaccination is recorded. The journey updates, the doctor sees it, and the next milestone opens.

Completion feeds the next gap. That is what keeps a pregnancy, a delivery and a child's first years on one continuous record instead of scattered across providers.

About Anvaya

Care is rarely missed on purpose

It slips when a report sits with one lab, a follow-up sits with another hospital, and nobody holds the whole picture — least of all the doctor with eight minutes on the clock.

One journey, from the first antenatal visit onward

Every visit, investigation, supplement, scan and vaccination lands on a single timeline that knows which stage the family is in and what should happen next.

  • Stage-aware. The timeline reshapes itself at each trimester, at delivery, and again as the child grows.
  • Readable by anyone. Plain language, multilingual, with voice support for low-literacy users.
  • Caregivers included. A husband, mother-in-law or ASHA worker can be given consent-based access.

Your doctor sees the same record you do

Authorised clinicians open a patient timeline instead of a pile of paper — pending investigations, missed follow-ups, upcoming milestones and delivery readiness, all in one view.

  • Clinicians stay in charge. Doctors validate and adjust the care pathway; Anvaya coordinates around their decisions.
  • Ready before the delivery. Facilities can see expected cases, staff availability and equipment readiness in advance.
  • Minimum necessary data. Role-based access and audit logs, so each person sees only what their role requires.

Delivery is not the end of the journey

Anvaya splits into two linked journeys — the mother's recovery and each child's growth — so postpartum care does not quietly fall away once the baby arrives.

  • For the mother. Postpartum follow-ups, recovery, breastfeeding support and mental-health screening with referral.
  • For the child. Vaccinations, growth monitoring, developmental milestones and paediatric follow-ups.
  • Twins stay separate. Each child gets their own profile under the same family account, so records never mix.

Built for everyone in the loop

The same record, shown differently depending on who is looking at it and what they are allowed to see.

Mothers & caregivers

Current stage, upcoming milestones, reports, appointments, nearby services and recommended next steps — in their own language, by voice if reading is hard.

Doctors

A patient timeline with pending investigations, missed follow-ups and delivery readiness. Clinicians validate the pathway and keep the system clinically grounded.

Facilities & PHCs

Expected cases, service and staff availability, equipment readiness and authorised incoming patient information ahead of a delivery.

Anvaya does not practise medicine

No diagnosis, no prescriptions, and no declaring a pregnancy high-risk on its own. That judgement stays with qualified healthcare professionals — Anvaya coordinates care around their decisions and escalates to them when something needs attention.
